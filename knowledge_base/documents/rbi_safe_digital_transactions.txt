
TITLE: Reserve Bank sensitises members of public on safe use of digital transactions

SOURCE ORGANISATION: Reserve Bank of India

DATE: 22 June 2020

SOURCE URL:
https://www.rbi.org.in/CommonPerson/english/scripts/Notification.aspx?Id=3232

CONTENT:

Safety and security of digital transactions are of paramount importance to their users.

The Reserve Bank has undertaken digital awareness campaigns through print and audio-visual media, including its RBI Kehta Hai programme.

Fraudsters may lure users on fictitious pretexts, including alleged completion of KYC requirements and impersonating banks and payment system operators.

The RBI advises users:

1. Do not share ATM or card details.
2. Do not share passwords, PINs, OTPs, CVVs or UPI PINs.
3. Avoid financial transactions through public, open or free Wi-Fi networks.
4. Do not store important banking data on mobile phones, email or electronic wallets.
5. Banks and payment system operators never ask customers to disclose passwords, PINs, OTPs or CVVs.
