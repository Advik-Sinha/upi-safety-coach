
TITLE: Financial Awareness Messages - Customer Liability for Fraudulent Digital Transactions
SOURCE ORGANISATION: Reserve Bank of India (RBI)
SOURCE TYPE: Official RBI Financial Awareness Messages (FAME)
SOURCE URL: https://rbi.org.in/commonman/images/FAME202426022024.pdf

TOPIC: Unauthorised Digital Transactions and Customer Liability

CONTENT:

RBI advises customers to notify their bank immediately if they encounter a fraudulent or unauthorised electronic banking transaction.

NOTIFY YOUR BANK:

1. Notify your bank immediately if you encounter a fraudulent or unauthorised electronic banking transaction.
2. The longer you take to notify the bank, the higher the potential loss to you or your bank.
3. Your bank should provide an acknowledgement when you report the complaint.

BANK RESPONSIBILITY:

1. If fraudulent transactions continue after you have informed the bank, the bank will have to bear the loss.
2. The bank must resolve the complaint within the applicable period specified in RBI guidance.

CUSTOMER LIABILITY:

Customer liability depends on the circumstances of the transaction and applicable RBI directions.

IMPORTANT:

This is a curated summary of RBI guidance, not a verbatim reproduction. Do not assume that every unauthorised transaction has the same liability outcome.
