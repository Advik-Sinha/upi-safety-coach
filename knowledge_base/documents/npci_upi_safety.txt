
TITLE: MyUPI - User Controls and Safety Switch
SOURCE ORGANISATION: National Payments Corporation of India (NPCI)
SOURCE TYPE: Official NPCI product information
SOURCE URL: https://www.npci.org.in/product/myupi.html

TOPIC: UPI Safety Switch and Transaction Controls

CONTENT:

NPCI's MyUPI describes a Safety Switch that can request the decline of UPI debit transactions across selected linked accounts.

IMPORTANT LIMITATIONS:

1. Availability depends on access through a supported UPI app.
2. The Safety Switch is an additional safeguard.
3. It does not replace contacting the bank or issuer.
4. The bank or issuer should be contacted for transaction blocking, account-level controls and fraud reporting.

IMPORTANT:

Do not assume that every UPI application supports the Safety Switch. Users should check the features available in their own supported UPI application.

END OF DOCUMENT
