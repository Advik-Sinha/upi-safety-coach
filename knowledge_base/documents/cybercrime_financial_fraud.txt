
TITLE: Reporting Financial Cyber Fraud
SOURCE ORGANISATION: Indian Cyber Crime Coordination Centre (I4C), Ministry of Home Affairs
SOURCE TYPE: Official government portal FAQ and reporting guidance
SOURCE URL: https://cybercrime.gov.in/webform/FAQ.aspx

CONTENT:

The National Cyber Crime Reporting Portal allows citizens to report online financial fraud and other cybercrimes.

To report financial cyber fraud:
1. Visit https://cybercrime.gov.in/
2. Select Register a Complaint.
3. Choose Financial Fraud.
4. Select Register a Complaint and follow the portal's instructions.
5. Provide accurate incident details and relevant supporting evidence.
6. Save the complaint acknowledgement number for future reference.

The portal's Report Other Cybercrimes process requires registration using a valid Indian mobile number and OTP verification.

The official cybercrime helpline for financial cyber fraud is 1930.

Relevant evidence may include transaction receipts, bank statements, emails, chat transcripts, website URLs and screenshots.

Complaints are handled by the concerned State or Union Territory police authorities.

IMPORTANT:
Report financial cyber fraud promptly. Contact your bank immediately as well. Never share your UPI PIN, OTP or banking passwords while seeking assistance.
