
TITLE: Responding to Shared or Exposed UPI Payment Credentials

SOURCE ORGANISATION: Reserve Bank of India (RBI)
SOURCE TYPE: Curated summary of official RBI Financial Awareness Messages
SOURCE URL: https://rbi.org.in/commonman/images/FAME202426022024.pdf

TOPIC: Immediate Response to Exposed Payment Credentials

CONTENT:

If a customer has shared payment credentials, such as a password or PIN, and an unauthorised transaction occurs, RBI guidance highlights the importance of notifying the bank immediately.

IMMEDIATE ACTIONS:

1. Contact your bank immediately through its official website, app, phone banking service or other officially listed channel.
2. Explain that your payment credentials may have been exposed.
3. Report any unauthorised transaction to the bank without delay.
4. Ask the bank what steps are available to secure your account and UPI access.
5. Request and retain the complaint acknowledgement or reference number.
6. Review recent account transactions and report any other suspicious activity.

CUSTOMER LIABILITY:

RBI's Financial Awareness Messages explain that when a loss occurs due to customer negligence, such as sharing payment credentials, the customer bears the loss until the unauthorised transaction is reported to the bank.

The applicable liability depends on the circumstances. It should not be assumed that every case has the same outcome.

REPORTING CYBER FRAUD:

For suspected financial cyber fraud in India, users can also contact the national cyber fraud helpline at 1930 or use the National Cyber Crime Reporting Portal:

https://cybercrime.gov.in/

IMPORTANT SAFETY RULES:

- Never share your UPI PIN, OTP or banking password with anyone.
- Do not disclose credentials to someone claiming to be a bank employee.
- Never enter your UPI PIN merely to receive money.
- Contact your bank using independently verified official contact details.
